The cycle: the steps of this project

Declared under [cycle] in .git-board.toml, read from the base branch. Every project starts with the default seven; take steps away, add a sign-off per validation status, give a kind of issue its own list. A step's gate comes with its kind.
Steps · 9 · drag to orderA kind of issue skips steps it doesn't list; risk may skip Plan.
#KindNameOpens atRoleGate, done when
01Criteria under a heading; the PM approves the version. No issue, no agent.
02Low, normal or structural, written on the issue.
03Approved by the plan role; structural: its own PR first. Skipped at low risk.
04By kind: a bug ships a failing test, a feature names a test per criterion, a doc none.
05A person commits and opens the PR; the checks pass at its head.
06One approval; structural: a second human. The merge opens the next step.
07The criteria ticked on staging; the role's person signs off. Added from the validation status.
08The role's person signs off, with a note.
09The last sign-off closes the issue, and GitHub's with it.
Seven kinds, each with its workspace and its gate. A sign-off is the only kind you can have several times.

Written to the working tree's .git-board.toml, as Enable checks does; it counts for the gates once committed on the base branch. Issues in flight keep their step: the step comes from the status and the evidence, and a status that no step opens at shows "not in the cycle" under the card.
