19e · The bar

What moved up, and what stays down

The bar is the drawer's own line: where the issue is and what you can do with it as a whole. What describes the issue's state or work stays under the title.

Moved upWhy
#2810, and ClosedThe issue's identity, next to the view it came from: a breadcrumb.
GitHub, GitLab, their countsWhere else it lives; the same for every issue.
The bellYour relation to the issue, not the issue's state.
MoreEdit the title, copy the id, close the issue (also by the composer).
k, j, EscGone from view: in the tooltips and the names of their buttons.
Stays downWhy
The status and its arrowThe issue's main control: in the bar it would read as the drawer's.
The fields, the branchesThe issue's state and its work: they need the column's width.
ParticipantsThey belong to the conversation (16f).
The tabsThey switch the column under them.

What goes first as the drawer narrows: the link and Open as a page (into More), then the view's name shortens. The number, the forges, the bell and Close never go.

The blank beside the column

At 1024 the column keeps 40rem, so 12rem stays blank each side: it holds a comment's lines near 75 characters, the reading measure. The drawer is resizable, and at 40rem the blank is gone. A wider column would make the conversation's lines too long to read.
