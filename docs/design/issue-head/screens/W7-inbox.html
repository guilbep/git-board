The inbox bell

inboxcheck.ts bellState, InboxBell.vue: a bell in the text color when the issue reaches your inbox; a grey bell when a project role brings its creation and closing only; a grey bell-off when it doesn’t. Hover turns it red. The popover’s three radios set the follow on click (PUT /api/inbox/follow/{id}). Without an identity, no bell.

Following

Bell, --fg. “Inbox: following. Because: assignee · commented”.

New and closed only

Bell, --fg-subtle. “Inbox: new and closed only. As lead-dev of the project.”

Not following

Bell-off, grey. Or “You unfollowed it.”
